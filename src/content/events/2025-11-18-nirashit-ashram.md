Good Earth is the council's student-led social enterprise. It sells eco-friendly products such as diyas, Ganesh idols and pencils, and its profits fund work like this visit.

On 18 November 2025, students visited Nirashit Ashram near Pitraparvat to spend time with the residents. They donated blankets, sweaters, knee caps, socks and health necessities, and interacted with the elders there.

Good Earth gives students real-world exposure to entrepreneurial work while they sell products they have made themselves.
