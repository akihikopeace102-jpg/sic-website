Innoverse is the school's largest student-led event, an e-summit and techfest organised by One Choithram and hosted at Choithram School, Manik Bagh, Indore from 28 to 30 September 2026.

Ten events were offered across two parallel worlds:

- **E-Summit:** The Brand Reboot, Pitch Arena, Fort Knox Escape, Make & Sell, Trade Tremors.
- **Techfest:** Code Decode, Frontier-1, The Crossfire, Survivor, Science Vault.

Registration was free, limited to one event per participant.
