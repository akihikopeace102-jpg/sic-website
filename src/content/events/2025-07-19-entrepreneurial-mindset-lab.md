The boot camp, titled **“Entrepreneurial Mindset Lab”**, was organised under the coordination of Ms. Vinita Sisodiya together with the then Head of the School Innovation Council, Aaradhya Vaid. It was planned as twelve bimonthly sessions running from July to December.

The first session, **“Ignite the Spark”**, was held on 19 July 2025 at Choithram School, Manik Bagh.

- **40 students** took part, from Tirathbai Kalachand School, Choithram School Nipania, Choithram School International and Choithram School Manik Bagh.
- The session was conducted by **Prof. Dr. Gaurav Chaitanya**, Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, Acropolis Institute of Technology and Research, Indore, whose work includes more than 40 published research papers in RF and Microwave Engineering and a role in establishing the AICTE IDEA Lab.
- The format ran an icebreaker, videos challenging common myths about entrepreneurship, and closed with a quiz.
