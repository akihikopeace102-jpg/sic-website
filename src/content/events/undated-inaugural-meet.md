The inaugural meeting of the School Innovation Council set the tone for the year.

- **SIC Head Aaradhya Vaid** opened, stressing the responsibilities of council members and the value of originality, collaboration and leadership.
- **Prinjal Jain** and **Kriday Lalwani** spoke about teamwork, leadership and the spirit of innovation.
- **E-Cell Head Pranjal Choudhary** outlined the year's planned workshops and factory visits.
- Heads of Marketing, Operations, IT, PR and Administration introduced their teams.
- **I-Cell Head Sourish Tilwankar** and his deputy presented their newly formed team and upcoming technical initiatives.
- **Good Earth Head Tanish Vyas** covered sustainability programmes and workshops, with presentations from Production, Finance, Media & PR, Sales & Marketing, Operations and HR.
- The meeting closed with a preview of **Innoverse**, the school's largest student-led event, and announcements of an E-Summit and a Techfest.
