**Objective for the month:** providing mentoring and using the existing infrastructure of the school to nurture innovation in learners.

**Planned activities for November:**

- Innovation Contest: business model development. Students develop the business plan for ideas submitted and compare Indian start-ups on business models using the case studies named in the calendar.
- Preparation of the CBSE Science Exhibition.
- Scholastic internships.
- MANAK awards, and a guideline draft for the MANAK awards.
- Laboratory visit.
- Hackathon, ideathon or robotics competition.
- Guideline for building an innovation culture in schools.

Dates, times and venues for these activities will be announced.
