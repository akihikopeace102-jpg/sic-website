On 12 July 2025, council members visited Shakti Pumps India Ltd at Pithampur to see how basic raw material is transformed into technologically advanced, packaged products.

What they were shown:

- **Evaluation parameters for pump systems:** depth level, bore size and discharge capacity.
- **Solar pumps as the commercial centre of the business,** accounting for over 60% of the company's total sales.
- **Components:** solar modules, batteries and drives (VED), and how each contributes to sustainable function.
- **The manufacturing floor:** dedicated blocks for each phase, from hydraulic press machines and fabricated sheets to assemblies of shafts, rotors, stators and impellers.
- **Policy context:** alignment with the PM-KUSUM Yojana, and an innovation record of 14 granted and 22 pending patents.

The visit closed on the company's growth from a small unit in 1982 into one of India's largest pump exporters, and on the interest it sparked among members in real-world industrial solutions.
