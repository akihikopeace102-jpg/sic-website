On 26 August 2025, Maharaja Ranjit Singh School hosted the Junior Scientist Competition, with participating schools presenting solutions for a sustainable future.

Choithram School was represented by **Manasvi Patangia** and **Dhruvee Nema**, both of Class IX A, with their project **“Svasthaa – Eco Herbal Pads”**. Their model proposed a safe, biodegradable alternative to conventional sanitary products, addressing both menstrual health and environmental responsibility.

The project won the **Best Model Presentation Award** along with a cash prize of **₹1,000**, and all participants received certificates.
