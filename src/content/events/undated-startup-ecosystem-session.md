The Entrepreneurship Cell organised a session on the start-up ecosystem for its members, with **Mr. Nitil Gupta**, a startup mentor and innovation strategist with over 20 years of entrepreneurial experience, currently associated with Supercharger Ventures and PIEDS (BITS Pilani).

Attended by all E-Cell members and E-Ship students, the session covered what makes a startup successful, the RSI framework, and practical steps to building a venture, illustrated with real-life examples and founder stories.

Students left with sharper skills in problem identification, opportunity evaluation, strategic risk-taking and turning ideas into actionable business models.
