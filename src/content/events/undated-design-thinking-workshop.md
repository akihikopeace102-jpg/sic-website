The workshop was run under the mentorship of **Mr. Anukrit Saxena**, Founder of Latent Brush, Indore.

- **First day:** students interacted with the mentor and learned about design thinking and ideation; he shared his own journey and experiences.
- **Second day:** students addressed 22 real-world problems, developed ideas and presented short overviews.
- **Third day:** students pitched complete business ideas with SWOT analysis, marketing strategies, ethical values and financial planning.

Winning ideas: **Clannet** (women safety service and product), **Starlyncs** (skill and employment platform) and **TerraCare** (eco-friendly sanitary pad disposal system).
