The Plantation Drive is an initiative of Good Earth, the council's student-led social enterprise. The drive held on 11 August 2026 recorded three figures:

- **30** participants.
- **20** saplings planted and watered.
- A **50-minute** awareness rally, from 12:00 pm to 12:50 pm, opening the event.

During the rally, participants raised awareness with sustainability-themed slogans and displayed posters promoting environmental conservation. The aim was to instil a sense of collective responsibility towards the surroundings.

Good Earth funds work like this itself. It sells eco-friendly products such as diyas, Ganesh idols, pencils, compost and saplings, and the proceeds have covered community visits and donations.
