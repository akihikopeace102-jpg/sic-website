Students of Classes 9 and 11 from Good Earth visited an orphanage in Kalani Nagar along with Mrs. Sangeeta Gupta and Mrs. Swati Manjrekar.

The children welcomed the team with a morning prayer and a welcome song. Our students spent time playing antakshari and dumb charades, and talking with them.

The team distributed foodgrains, chocolates, woollen caps and Good Earth pencils, all of them provided through the profits earned by Good Earth.
