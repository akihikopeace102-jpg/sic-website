On 18 August 2025, Grade 9 students at Choithram School, Manik Bagh attended a guest lecture on entrepreneurship by **Mr. Himanshu Khale**, an entrepreneur and alumnus of the school.

The session opened with an interactive discussion and then moved through entrepreneurship fundamentals, the qualities entrepreneurs need, the significance of calculated risk-taking, and the role of a CEO. The lecture offered practical guidance on turning ideas into tangible opportunities.
