Good Earth hosted its inaugural eco-friendly sale, offering bookmarks, key chains, pencil seeds, compost and saplings. The stock sold out within minutes, with strong interest from students of Classes IX to XII.

Japanese exchange students visiting the school explored the stalls and bought bookmarks and key chains. Compost and saplings were purchased by guests judging another event running at the same time. The sale ended with little compost or sapling stock left.

**11 girls, 5 boys and one teacher** took part, and the sale brought five emerging student leaders forward.
