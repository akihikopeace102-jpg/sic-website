The council ran a week-long ideation awareness drive in the last week of July 2025. Thirteen student members from Grades 10 to 12 conducted the sessions themselves, across Grades 6 to 12.

**What the sessions covered:** core ideation concepts, including identifying problems, writing effective problem statements, and developing prototypes, in a hands-on, participative format.

**Impact:** enthusiastic participation from the audience, and presentation experience for the student speakers. The council intends to continue the format.
